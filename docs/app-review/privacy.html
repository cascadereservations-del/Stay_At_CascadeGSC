Privacy Policy

Cascade Hideaway, Bria Homes, Conel Road, Barangay San Isidro, General Santos City, Philippines. Effective 12 September 2026.

Cascade Hideaway is a private guest unit. This policy explains what we collect when you message us on Facebook Messenger, book through our website, or stay with us, and what we do with it.

Messenger concierge

When you message our Facebook Page, an automated concierge may answer routine questions (rates, availability, amenities, directions). To do that we store your Messenger page-scoped ID, the first name Facebook shares with the Page, the text of the conversation, and the times of the messages. Questions the concierge cannot handle are shown to our hosts, who reply personally.

Message text is sent to an AI language model provider (Google Gemini, with OpenRouter as a fallback) solely to draft a reply. We keep the last 24 messages of a conversation for context and drop older ones automatically.

Bookings and stays

To confirm a reservation we collect the names of the guests, a contact number, a valid government ID for each adult guest (required by our community's gate security), and payment confirmation. Payment details are handled by GCash, UnionBank or Airbnb; we never store card or account numbers.

Where the data lives

Our records are stored in a Supabase database hosted in the Singapore region, protected by access controls and encryption in transit. Facebook processes Messenger data under Meta's own privacy policy.

Retention

Conversation history: up to 12 months after the last message, or sooner on request. Booking records: as long as required for accounting under Philippine law, then deleted. Government ID copies: deleted within 30 days after check-out.

Your rights

You may ask us at any time to show, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or to stop the automated concierge from replying to you. Email cascadereservations@gmail.com or message the Page and ask for a host. We answer within 7 days.

Data deletion

To delete your Messenger data, send the message "delete my data" to the Page or email the address above. We confirm once the deletion is complete.
