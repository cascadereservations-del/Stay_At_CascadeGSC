Boundaries that stay fixed

Decisions already locked

Finance is never OPS.

OPS excludes money, receipts, bank data, payment status, rates, deposits, refunds and guest contact details.

AI is advisory.

Models may extract, classify, draft and flag. They never confirm funds, approve bookings, override inspection, or publish without authority.

Purchases stop at approval.

Recommendations and shopping lists are allowed; automatic supplier ordering is not.

n8n is delivery only.

Supabase owns booking, calendar, payment, finance, inventory and approval state.
