Immediate build contract

Module C decision chain

Every machine-produced signal stops at advice. Only a named authorized Finance reviewer can cross the approval boundary.

Step 1 Evidence candidate

Receipt, OCR, model advice, allowlisted mail, or manual evidence.

Step 2 Deterministic comparison

Record match, mismatch, duplicate, ambiguity, or missing fields.

Step 3 Finance review record

Immutable provenance, outcome, reason, reviewer, and time.

Step 4 Named human approval

Authorized Finance identity acts explicitly; service identities cannot.

Step 5 Booking decision RPC

Canonical atomic transition; delivery stays outside the transaction.

OCR, OpenRouter, receipt evidence, bank email, or deterministic comparison alone must never confirm payment or a booking.
