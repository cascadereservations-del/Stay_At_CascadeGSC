# Personal-data breach response

1. **Contain:** revoke exposed token/session, disable workflow or endpoint, preserve relevant logs, and stop further disclosure. Do not delete evidence.
2. **Assess:** identify systems, categories, approximate subjects, duration, access likelihood, and whether payment/access/safety information was involved.
3. **Document:** timestamp discovery, containment actions, decision owner, evidence location, and open unknowns. Redact secrets from the incident record.
4. **Decide notification:** owner obtains current legal/privacy advice before deciding whether regulatory or individual notification is required. Do not assume a deadline from this runbook.
5. **Recover:** rotate credentials, patch root cause, test the fix, perform a least-privilege review, and record follow-up tasks.

High-priority examples include public receipt links, staff-session compromise, wrong Telegram route, exposed access code, and unauthorized n8n credential use.
