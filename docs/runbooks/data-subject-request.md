# Data subject request runbook

1. Verify requester identity without asking them to send unnecessary identity documents by chat.
2. Open an internal request with type (access, correction, deletion), scope, requester-contact method, and received time.
3. Search only the relevant systems: Supabase records/storage, approved Gmail evidence metadata, and delivery audit logs. Do not export unrelated guests or staff data.
4. For correction, retain an audit note of the changed field and actor.
5. For deletion, check legal/dispute/payment holds before anonymizing/deleting. Never silently break booking, ledger, or safety audit links.
6. Record the decision, records affected, hold basis where applicable, and response date.
7. Escalate uncertain scope, identity, legal, or safety requests to the owner before action.

This is an operational procedure, not a promise of a statutory response period. Confirm current Philippine requirements before public-facing commitments.
