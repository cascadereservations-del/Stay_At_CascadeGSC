Privacy notice

This notice explains how Cascade Reservations ("Cascade Hideaway", "we", "us"), operator of the Cascade Hideaway short-term rental in General Santos City, Philippines, collects and uses information when you submit a direct booking request through this website. This notice has not been independently certified as legally compliant; it is a plain-language description of our current practice.

What we collect

FieldWhy we ask
NameTo identify your reservation and address you personally.
Phone numberTo confirm your stay and reach you about check-in.
Email addressTo send your acknowledgement receipt and confirmation.
Requested datesTo check availability and calculate your rate.
Notes you provideTo accommodate reasonable requests (early check-in, accessibility, etc.).
Payment proof (screenshot/receipt)To verify your reservation-fee or full payment.

How we use it

We use your details only to process and manage this stay: checking availability, verifying payment, sending your confirmation, and communicating about your booking. We do not sell your information. We do not use it for marketing unless you separately and explicitly opt in to hear about future offers — transactional booking messages are never conditioned on marketing consent.

ProviderRole

n8nSends booking notifications and reminders on our behalf from server-side events; it never receives your data directly from your browser.

Retention

Booking records are retained for as long as needed for guest-history, tax, and dispute-resolution purposes, and are then deleted or anonymized. Payment receipt images are retained only as long as needed to verify the associated booking and are stored in a private, access-controlled location — not a public link.

Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you, or to explain how it is used, by emailing cascadereservations@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Third-party proof content

This site may display a live embedded preview of our official Airbnb listing to show current guest reviews. If loaded, that embed is provided directly by Airbnb and may set its own cookies or make its own network requests, governed by Airbnb's own privacy policy. We do not scrape, proxy, or store Airbnb's review content ourselves.
