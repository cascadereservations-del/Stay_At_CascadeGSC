How we use it

We use your details only to process and manage this stay: checking availability, verifying payment, sending your confirmation, and communicating about your booking. We do not sell your information. We do not use it for marketing unless you separately and explicitly opt in to hear about future offers — transactional booking messages are never conditioned on marketing consent.

Messenger concierge

When you message our Facebook Page, an automated concierge may answer routine questions (rates, availability, amenities, directions, house rules). To do that we store your Messenger page-scoped ID, the first name Facebook shares with the Page, the text of the conversation, and the times of the messages. We keep the last 24 messages of a conversation for context and drop older ones automatically. Questions the concierge cannot handle (payments, cancellations, complaints, safety, price requests) are shown to our hosts, who reply personally.

To stop the concierge from replying to you, ask for a host in the chat. To delete your Messenger data, send the message "delete my data" to the Page or email cascadereservations@gmail.com; we confirm once the deletion is complete.

Retention

Messenger conversation history is kept for up to 12 months after the last message, or deleted sooner on request. Booking records are retained for as long as needed for guest-history, tax, and dispute-resolution purposes, and are then deleted or anonymized. Payment receipt images are retained only as long as needed to verify the associated booking and are stored in a private, access-controlled location — not a public link.

Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you, or to explain how it is used, by emailing cascadereservations@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Third-party proof content

This site may display a live embedded preview of our official Airbnb listing to show current guest reviews. If loaded, that embed is provided directly by Airbnb and may set its own cookies or make its own network requests, governed by Airbnb's own privacy policy. We do not scrape, proxy, or store Airbnb's review content ourselves.
