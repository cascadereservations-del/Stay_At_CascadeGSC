Booking terms

These terms describe how a direct booking with Cascade Hideaway in General Santos City works, from request to check-out.

What's included

The quoted nightly rate always includes cleaning — there is no separate cleaning fee added at checkout.

Occupancy

The unit accommodates up to 3 guests (1 bedroom, 1 queen bed, 1 floor mattress, 1 bath). Day visitors are welcome with advance notice; overnight guests beyond the declared headcount are not permitted.

Check-in / check-out

Check-in begins at 2:00 PM. Check-out is at 12:00 PM (noon). Early check-in or late check-out may be arranged depending on availability — mention it in your booking request.

How a reservation request becomes a confirmed booking

Submitting the booking form sends a reservation request, not a confirmed booking. We personally match your submitted details against your payment and current availability, then send your confirmation directly — by email, WhatsApp, or Messenger. Your dates are not confirmed until that message arrives.

Deposit and payment timing

A 50% deposit secures your requested dates once verified. The remaining balance is settled at check-in. All payments go directly to the property host — GCash, UnionBank InstaPay/PESONet, or cash on arrival. There is no third-party payment processor.

Payment verification

After sending payment, attach a screenshot or receipt in the booking form (optional at submission time, but required before your stay is confirmed). We review payment proof personally; screenshots are stored privately and are not publicly accessible.

Security deposit

A refundable security deposit may be requested at check-in to cover damage, excess cleaning, or policy violations, and is returned after checkout inspection finds no issues.

Cancellation and refunds

  • 7 or more days before check-in: full refund.
  • 3–6 days before check-in: 50% of the deposit is returned.
  • Under 3 days before check-in: the booking is fully honoured in place; no refund is issued for a late cancellation.

Disputes

If you believe a charge, refund, or booking decision was made in error, contact us at cascadereservations@gmail.com with your booking reference. We aim to resolve disputes directly and promptly; unresolved payment disputes may also be raised with your bank or e-wallet provider under its own dispute process.
