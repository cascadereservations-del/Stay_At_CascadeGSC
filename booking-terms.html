What's included

The quoted nightly rate always includes cleaning — there is no separate cleaning fee added at checkout.

Occupancy

The unit accommodates up to 3 guests (1 bedroom, 1 queen bed, 1 single pull-out bed (underbed), 1 bath). Day visitors are welcome with advance notice; overnight guests beyond the declared headcount are not permitted.

Check-in / check-out

Check-in begins at 2:00 PM. Check-out is at 12:00 PM (noon). Early check-in or late check-out may be arranged depending on availability — mention it in your booking request.

How a reservation request becomes a confirmed booking

Submitting the booking form sends a reservation request, not a confirmed booking. We personally match your submitted details against your payment and current availability, then send your confirmation directly — by email, WhatsApp, or Messenger. Your dates are not confirmed until that message arrives.

Payment and timing

Every direct stay is paid in full to confirm. There is no part payment, so nothing is left to settle later. Five or more days before check-in, submitting the form holds your dates for 24 hours while you send your payment; if nothing arrives, the hold is released and nothing is owed. All payments go directly to the property host: GCash or UnionBank InstaPay/PESONet. There is no third-party payment processor.

Stays of 28 nights or more are arranged with the host: the first month is paid to confirm, then monthly. Please message us on Messenger or e-mail cascadereservations@gmail.com and we will set it up with you.

Payment verification

After sending payment, attach a screenshot or receipt in the booking form (optional at submission time, but required before your stay is confirmed). We review payment proof personally; screenshots are stored privately and are not publicly accessible.

Security deposit

A separate, refundable security deposit of ₱800 is paid with your booking or by the day before check-in. It is not part of the stay amount. It covers damage, excess cleaning, or policy violations. To keep the property secure, your door PIN is sent once your payment, IDs and the deposit are in. You may send the deposit together with your payment (the stay total plus ₱800) or on its own.

The deposit is returned as soon as possible, within 3 business days of check-out, to the same account or payment mode it came from. Airbnb bookings carry no security deposit through this site.

Cancellation and refunds

  • Free cancellation until 5 days before check-in, the same as our Airbnb listing.
  • Changed your mind within 24 hours of paying? Full refund, when check-in is at least 7 days away.
  • You may move your stay once, free, up to 5 days before check-in, subject to availability.
  • Inside 5 days, or for a no-show, we keep 50% of the stay and refund the rest. Your security deposit is returned in full.
  • If a government-declared calamity in General Santos or an airport closure stops your trip, choose new dates or a full refund.
  • Refunds go to the GCash or bank account you paid from, within 3 business days. To cancel or change, use the link in your booking message or e-mail cascadereservations@gmail.com.
  • Airbnb bookings follow Airbnb's own cancellation policy.

Disputes

If you believe a charge, refund, or booking decision was made in error, contact us at cascadereservations@gmail.com with your booking reference. We aim to resolve disputes directly and promptly; unresolved payment disputes may also be raised with your bank or e-wallet provider under its own dispute process.
